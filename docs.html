01 · OVERVIEWWhat is Save Cat

A green low poly cat who lives in a bedroom from a life sim released in 2002. Someone saved the game on 14 March 2003 and never loaded it again. The cat is still in that save, sitting on the carpet, waiting for Continue.

The site is that memory card slot. One window, the room behind it, the cat in the middle. Every screenshot on the card is the same cat loaded into another old game by mistake.

CHARACTER
One green cat, faceted like a PlayStation 2 model, calm and unimpressed
PLACE
Bedroom, slot 1, room 1, the blinds are always half open
CHAIN
Robinhood Chain, token through the Pons launchpad
SITE
One page, no framework, the video behind the window is the room breathing

02 · SAVE DATAThe room

Nothing in it has moved since 2003. The cat counts this as a feature.

room 1 ├── bed blue plaid, never slept in, the cat sits next to it ├── desk one CRT monitor, one lamp, one mug that is not his ├── shelf action figures facing the wall, they lost ├── carpet grey, the cat's spot is exactly in the middle ├── window blinds half open, the light outside never changes └── door closed, opened once in 2003 to save the game

09 · FAQQuestions

Why is the cat greenThat is the colour he was saved in. Nobody has patched it
Can I load the saveNo. Continue is not on this card. You can look at the room and that is the whole game
Why does delete not workThe card is write protected, or the cat is, nobody has checked which
Is this a real gameThe room looks like one, the cat looks like one, none of it shipped. It only exists on this card
What happens at launchThe contract goes into the CA strip, the buy button starts pointing at the token page, the cat does not react
